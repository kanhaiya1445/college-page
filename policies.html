Our Hotel Policies

Important information for all guests staying at Kavya Residency.

At Kavya Residency, we want every guest to enjoy a safe, comfortable and pleasant stay. Please read the following policies before making your reservation.

🕑

Check-in & Check-out

Our standard timings are designed to make arrival and departure convenient.

  • Check-in: 2:00 PM
  • Check-out: 11:00 AM
  • Early check-in is subject to availability.
  • Late check-out may involve additional charges.
📅

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms may vary depending on the room type, rate and booking offer.

  • Check your booking confirmation for specific terms.
  • Some promotional rates may be non-refundable.
  • Late cancellations may attract charges.
  • No-show bookings may be charged according to the selected rate.
💳

Payment Policy

Payment requirements depend on the selected room and booking option.

  • Advance payment may be required for selected bookings.
  • Accepted payment methods may include cards and online payments.
  • Additional hotel services may be charged separately.
  • Final charges will be shown during booking or checkout.

Children Policy

Families are welcome at Kavya Residency.

  • Children are welcome.
  • Additional charges may apply depending on age and room occupancy.
  • Extra beds are subject to availability.
  • Please mention children's requirements while booking.
🐾

Pet Policy

Pet availability depends on hotel rules and selected room type.

  • Please contact the hotel before travelling with a pet.
  • Pets may not be permitted in certain areas.
  • Additional cleaning or service charges may apply.
  • Guests are responsible for their pets.
🚭

Smoking Policy

We maintain a comfortable environment for all our guests.

  • Smoking restrictions apply inside designated areas.
  • Please use designated smoking areas where provided.
  • Smoking in restricted rooms may result in additional cleaning charges.
🤝

Guest Conduct

Guests are expected to respect the property, staff and other guests.

  • Excessive noise is not permitted.
  • Damage to hotel property may result in charges.
  • Illegal activities are strictly prohibited.
  • Guests must follow hotel safety instructions.

Safety & Security

Guest safety is one of our highest priorities.

  • 24/7 security services are available.
  • Please keep valuables secured.
  • Report suspicious activity to hotel staff.
  • Follow emergency instructions when required.

Important Information

Policies may vary depending on your selected room, rate plan, package or special offer.
